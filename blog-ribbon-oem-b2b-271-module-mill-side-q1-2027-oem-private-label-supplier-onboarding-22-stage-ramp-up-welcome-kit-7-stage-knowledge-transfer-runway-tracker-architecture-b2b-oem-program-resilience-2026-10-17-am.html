4. Stage 5-6 — Pre-Production PPAP, Pre-Shipment AQL, and Inline Quality Stack

Day 36 to Day 70 is pre-production and pre-shipment QA. PPAP (Production Part Approval Process) is built around 18 elements: design records, authorized engineering change documents, customer-specific requirements, design FMEA, process flow, PFMEA, control plan, MSA, capability indices (Cpk > 1.33), initial process studies, dimensional results, material certifications, lab-test reports (wash, rub, light, perspiration, crocking), appearance-AQL photo stack, bulk-PPAP sample, sample production lot, sample measurement equipment, and customer-specific bonus reports. Pre-shipment AQL uses MIL-STD-105E Level II: 32-piece sample for lots up to 1,200 m, 50-piece for up to 3,200 m, 80-piece for up to 10,000 m. Inline AI-vision defect detection on the mill's loom catches 96 percent of weft-bar, slub, stain, and misweave defects at 0.4 second latency, replacing the legacy manual-loom-side-spot-check approach that missed 18 to 28 percent of critical defects.
