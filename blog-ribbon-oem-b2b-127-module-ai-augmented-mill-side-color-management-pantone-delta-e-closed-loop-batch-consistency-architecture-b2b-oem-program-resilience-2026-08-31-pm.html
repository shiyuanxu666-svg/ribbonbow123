How Smith Ribbon Operationalises the 127-Module AI-Augmented Mill-Side Color-Management, Pantone & Delta-E Closed-Loop Batch-Consistency Program — 11-Step Audit, 6-Stakeholder RACI, 14-Clause Rider, 7-Stage Ladder, Quarterly Recalc

Smith Ribbon runs the 127-module AI-augmented mill-side color-management, Pantone & Delta-E closed-loop batch-consistency program as a quarterly cadence. Step 1 Target-Color-Definition: the team receives the brand-buyer Pantone reference (TPG, TCX, TPX, FHIP), locks the illuminant (D65 / A / F2 / D50) and the observer-angle (2° / 10°), and archives the master in the brand-color-database. Step 2 Substrate-Characterization & Dye-Class-Selection: the team characterizes the substrate (polyester, satin, grosgrain, organza, velvet, RPET) and selects the matching dye-class (disperse, acid, reactive, vat, pigment) for the substrate. Step 3 Aux-Recipe & Process-Curve: the team formulates the aux-recipe (surfactant, dispersing, leveling, fixing) and maps the process-curve (temperature, time, pH, liquor-ratio, heating-rate, cooling-rate). Step 4 Lab-Trial & Recipe-Optimization: the team runs lab-trial-recipe-1, lab-trial-recipe-2, optimizes the recipe against target, runs recipe-cost-optimization, and recipe-stability-test. Step 5 Recipe-Lock & Database-Update: the team locks the approved recipe in the brand-color-database and exposes the recipe to the brand-buyer. Step 6 Pantone-Matching & Spectrophotometer-Reading: the team validates the Pantone reference against the actual ribbon substrate under locked illuminant, observer-angle, and runs spectrophotometer reading + Delta-E calculation. Step 7 Visual-Confirm & Brand-Buyer-Written-Approval: the team does visual-confirm under D65, archives the visual assessment, and obtains brand-buyer written-approval. Step 8 Lab-Dip-Approval Workflow: the team runs 3-day lab-dip, 5-day sub-dip, 7-day production-dip, and obtains brand-buyer written-approval before bulk production. Step 9 Delta-E Closed-Loop & Auto-Recipe-Adjustment: the team runs 13-stage Delta-E closed-loop (pre-dye, dye-liquor, in-process, post-dye, post-wash, post-heat-set, post-finish, post-slit, final, dE-2000, tolerance-check, auto-recipe-adjustment, brand-buyer-dE-report) per batch. Step 10 Batch-Consistency Ladder & Audit: the team tracks within-batch, between-batch, within-lot, between-lot, within-mill, between-mill, and brand-buyer-reference-approval and exposes the ladder per batch. Step 11 Compliance Mapping & Quarterly Recalc: the team maps every color output to ISO-105-B02, ISO-105-C06, ISO-105-X12, AATCC-TM16, AATCC-TM61, AATCC-TM8, Pantone-FHIP, Pantone-TPG, and CIE-dE-2000, recalculates the recipe-cost, recipe-substitution, recipe-stability, and brand-color-database every quarter, and publishes the new disclosure.
