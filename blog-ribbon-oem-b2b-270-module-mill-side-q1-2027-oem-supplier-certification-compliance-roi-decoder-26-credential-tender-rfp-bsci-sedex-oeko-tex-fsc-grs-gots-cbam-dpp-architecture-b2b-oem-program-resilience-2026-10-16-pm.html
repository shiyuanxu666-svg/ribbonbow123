1. Credential 1-7 — BSCI, SEDEX-SMETA, OEKO-TEX Standard 100, OEKO-TEX STEP, FSC, GRS, GOTS — Social-Compliance + Material-Sustainability Decoder

Credential 1 is BSCI (Business Social Compliance Initiative) where the mill-side factory is audited on 11 performance areas covering child-labor, forced-labor, freedom-of-association, discrimination, fair-remuneration, working-hours, occupational-health-safety, no-harsh-treatment, no-child-labor, special-protection-young-workers, and supply-chain-management discipline. Credential 2 is SEDEX-SMETA where the mill-side factory completes a 4-pillar SMETA audit covering labor-rights, health-safety, environment, and business-ethics discipline. Credential 3 is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 where every ribbon SKU is tested against 100+ harmful-substance limits covering azo-dyes, formaldehyde, heavy-metals, phthalates, and PFAS. Credential 4 is OEKO-TEX STEP where the mill-side factory is audited on 6 modules covering chemicals-management, environmental-performance, environmental-management, social-responsibility, quality-management, and safety discipline. Credential 5 is FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) where any paper-based ribbon packaging carries the FSC-chain-of-custody claim. Credential 6 is GRS (Global Recycled Standard) where any recycled-polyester (rPET) ribbon carries the GRS-claim with full chain-of-custody traceability. Credential 7 is GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard) where any organic-cotton or natural-fiber ribbon carries the GOTS-certification covering 70 percent to 100 percent organic-fiber content.

2. Credential 8-14 — ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, WRAP, RBA, ICSCA, C2C-Gold — Quality + ESG + Circular Decoder

Credential 8 is ISO 9001 where the mill-side factory runs the 10-clause quality-management-system covering leadership, planning, support, operation, performance-evaluation, and improvement discipline. Credential 9 is ISO 14001 where the mill-side factory runs the 10-clause environmental-management-system covering environmental-policy, planning, support, operation, performance-evaluation, and improvement discipline. Credential 10 is ISO 45001 where the mill-side factory runs the 7-clause occupational-health-and-safety-management-system covering leadership-worker-participation, planning, support, operation, performance-evaluation, and improvement discipline. Credential 11 is WRAP (Worldwide Responsible Accredited Production) where the mill-side factory is audited on 12 principles covering human-resources, health-safety, environmental-compliance, legal-compliance, and security-discipline. Credential 12 is RBA (Responsible Business Alliance) where the mill-side factory is audited on the RBA-Code-of-Conduct covering labor, health-safety, environment, and management-system discipline. Credential 13 is ICSCA (International Council of Chemical Associations) where the mill-side factory runs the chemicals-management-discipline covering responsible-care, product-stewardship, and supply-chain-communication discipline. Credential 14 is C2C-Gold (Cradle-to-Cradle-Gold) where the mill-side factory is certified on the 5-criteria C2C-Gold standard covering material-health, material-reutilization, renewable-energy, water-stewardship, and social-fairness discipline.

3. Credential 15-21 — SBTi, CDP, CSRD-ESRS-E1, EU-CBAM-Phase-2, DPP-Digital-Product-Passport, ESPR-Readiness, RSL-REACH-CPSIA-Prop-65 Compliance

Credential 16 is CDP (Carbon-Disclosure-Project) where the mill-side factory discloses its climate-change, water-security, and forests-disclosure data to the CDP-platform for brand-buyer transparency. Credential 17 is CSRD-ESRS-E1 where the mill-side factory discloses its Corporate-Sustainability-Reporting-Directive climate-change disclosure under the European-Union ESRS-E1 standard. Credential 18 is EU-CBAM-Phase-2 where the mill-side factory is registered as a CBAM-declarant and files the embedded-emissions data for every shipment entering the EU-market. Credential 19 is DPP (Digital-Product-Passport) where the mill-side factory generates a DPP-record for every ribbon SKU containing the material-composition, the carbon-footprint, the recyclability, and the supply-chain-traceability data. Credential 20 is ESPR-Readiness where the mill-side factory is audited on the Ecodesign-for-Sustainable-Products-Regulation readiness covering durability, repairability, recyclability, and material-efficiency discipline. Credential 21 is RSL-REACH-CPSIA-Prop-65 Compliance where the mill-side factory runs the Restricted-Substances-List compliance against REACH (EU), CPSIA (US), and California-Prop-65 (US-CA) harmful-substance regulations.
