The 9-Brief-Discovery Layer, 8-Swatch-Board Build & 11-Color-Approval Workflow

The 9-brief-discovery layer: Layer 1 — Brand Guideline Review (logo, font, color, mood): Brand-side. Layer 2 — End-Use Context (gift, beauty, holiday, floral, e-commerce): Required. Layer 3 — Target Cost (per-meter, per-SKU, target MSRP): Required. Layer 4 — Target Lead-Time (NPI, production, replenishment): Required. Layer 5 — MOQ Tier (1K / 3K / 5K / 10K / 30K / 100K+): Required. Layer 6 — Compliance Stack (OEKO-TEX, GRS, BSCI, FSC): Required. Layer 7 — Volume Forecast (12-24 month, season mix): Required. Layer 8 — Distribution Channel (retail, e-com, club, B2B): Required. Layer 9 — Sustainability Target (recycled, bio, carbon, mono-material): Required. The 8-swatch-board build: Swatch 1 — Yarn / Fiber (polyester, cotton, linen, RPET): 5-10 options. Swatch 2 — Weave (satin, grosgrain, organza, velvet, jacquard): 5-8 options. Swatch 3 — Pantone / Lab-Dip (Coated / Uncoated, custom): 8-16 colors. Swatch 4 — Width (3mm-100mm, standard + custom): 6-10 widths. Swatch 5 — Finish (matte, gloss, soft, stiff, wire-edge): 4-6 finishes. Swatch 6 — Print (logo, pattern, gradient, foil, emboss): 4-6 techniques. Swatch 7 — Edge (hot-cut, ultrasonic, merrowed, woven): 3-4 edges. Swatch 8 — Pack (ream, spool, header card, OPP bag, gift-pack): 4-6 packs. The 11-color-approval workflow: Step 1 — Pantone Translation (CMYK / RGB / Pantone C / U): 1 day. Step 2 — Lab-Dip Round 1 (yarn + dye + finish): 4-7 days. Step 3 — Lab-Dip Round 2 (correction): 4-7 days. Step 4 — Lab-Dip Round 3 (final): 4-7 days. Step 5 — Pantone Sign-Off (Delta-E <1.0): Brand-side. Step 6 — Light-Fastness Test (ISO 105-B02, 4-6 grade): 7 days. Step 7 — Wash-Fastness Test (ISO 105-C06, 3-4 grade): 5 days. Step 8 — Rub-Fastness Test (ISO 105-X12, 3-4 grade): 3 days. Step 9 — Sublimation Test (polyester only, 4-5 grade): 5 days. Step 10 — Color Approval Document (CAD, retention): Audit-trail. Step 11 — Master Color Standard (lot retention, 24-month): Brand-side.
