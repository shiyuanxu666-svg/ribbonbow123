Who We Are

Smith Ribbon is the export brand of Xiamen Meisida Decoration Co., Ltd. (厦门美丝达饰品有限公司) and Xiamen Smith Ribbon & Bow Co., Ltd. (厦门思蜜丝织带饰品有限公司). We are a vertically integrated OEM/ODM ribbon and decorative bow manufacturer headquartered in Xiamen, Fujian Province, China.

Since 2004, we have produced custom satin, grosgrain, organza, velvet, and specialty ribbons for global brands in gift packaging, festive decoration, apparel, hair accessories, and home décor. Our 15,000㎡ in-house factory runs 50+ production lines, enabling daily output of 100,000 meters with full control from yarn sourcing to finished, packed, and shipped goods.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Real factory, not a trading company. We own every step — weaving, dyeing, printing, bow-tying, QC, packaging.
  • 20+ years of B2B export experience. We have shipped to 50+ countries and onboarded 1000+ clients.
  • Certified for global retail. OEKO-TEX Standard 100, BSCI, FSC, ISO 9001, SEDEX, SMETA.
  • Low MOQ with high flexibility. 500m for stock items, 1,000m for custom. Trial orders welcome.
  • Sustainability options. RPET recycled polyester, bamboo fiber, FSC-certified paper packaging.
