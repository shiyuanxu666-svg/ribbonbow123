The 261-module architecture operates as a single integrated value stream rather than a checklist of independent tasks. Module 1 through Module 7 cover the upstream feedstock layer: yarn-forward sourcing (RPET, BCI, organic cotton, recycled polyester), dye-house selection (disperse, reactive, acid, vat), chemical-kitchen management (ZDHC MRSL compliance), pre-treatment sequencing, color-kitchen Pantone Delta-E stewardship, and lab-dip cycle compression. Module 8 through Module 14 govern the conversion layer: weaving (jacquard, satin, grosgrain, twill, herringbone, velvet, organza), finishing (heat-setting, calendaring, softening, anti-static, water-repellent), converting (slitting, cut-length, spool-wind, ball-roll, die-cut), packaging (retail-ready, B2B bulk, mailer, gift-box, FSC kraft), warehouse (bonded, FTZ, climate-controlled), and quality-control (AQL inline, AOI vision, pre-shipment). Module 15 through Module 21 govern the downstream value layer: pilot-line scale, R&D sample atelier, capacity-allocation and Q4-cascade pre-booking, smart-factory IoT/SCADA visibility, energy-water-carbon footprint, sub-supplier tier-2 tier-3 risk monitoring, trade-compliance HS-code/FTA/CBAM/Section-301 engineering, DPP/ESPR digital product passport disclosure, and the capex-plan that funds the roadmap.

Each module exposes a standardized KPI panel so a brand-buyer tender team can compare mills on an apples-to-apples basis. Yarn-forward is scored on RPET-GRS share and traceability. Dye-house is scored on ZDHC MRSL and water-recycle rate. Weaving is scored on SMV (standard minute value) and OEE. Finishing is scored on energy intensity (kWh/kg). Converting is scored on yield-loss percentage. Packaging is scored on FSC-share and soy-ink share. Warehouse is scored on pick-pack-ship OTD. Lab is scored on lead-time for lab-dip and strike-off. Quality-control is scored on AQL pass-rate and chargeback-rate. Pilot-line is scored on speed-to-shelf. R&D is scored on innovation-pipeline strength. Capacity is scored on Q4-pre-booking fill-rate. Smart-factory is scored on OEE-visibility. Energy-water-carbon is scored on Scope-3 disclosure. Sub-supplier-risk is scored on tier-2 tier-3 audit coverage. Trade-compliance is scored on HS-code-engineering accuracy. DPP/ESPR is scored on readiness for digital product passport. Capex-plan is scored on roadmap-funding ratio. This level of granularity is what differentiates a strategic mill partner from a transactional converter.
