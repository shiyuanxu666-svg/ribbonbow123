Section 1 — The 9-Brief-Inception Layer & 8-Design-Concept Workflow

The 9-brief-inception layer covers 9 elements: Brand brief intake (creative, merchandising, procurement), Discovery call (30 min) with program lead, Volume forecast (SKU count, color count, total meter), Window mapping (retail launch, in-store date, e-commerce date), Risk register (capacity, material, color, IP, regulatory), Cost target (FOB, CIF, DDP landed cost), Quality spec (Delta E, AQL, fastness, OEKO-TEX, RPET, FSC), Packaging spec (mono-material, FSC, soy-ink, biodegradable tissue), Sustainability spec (GRS, CSRD, DPP, ZDHC). Brief-inception target: 1-2 working days from brand-RFP to signed statement-of-work. The 8-design-concept workflow covers 8 elements: Concept sketch (3-5 directions), Digital mockup (Pantone, finish, edge, hot-stamp, emboss), AI-assisted variation generation, Customer-side design review (2-3 rounds), Final design freeze (hand-off to material-sourcing), Color-palette finalization (Pantone TCX / TPG), Artwork vectorization (Adobe Illustrator), Print-readiness preflight (DPI, bleed, color-space). Design-cycle target: 3-5 working days from brief to design-freeze. The 9-and-8 brief-to-design is the operating spine.

Section 2 — The 7-Material-Sourcing Tier & 8-Color-Lab-Dip Cycle

The 7-material-sourcing tier covers 7 elements: Yarn stock check (greige, dyed, RPET, bamboo, organic cotton), Dye stuff allocation (Pantone match, fastness grade), Auxiliaries allocation (leveler, softener, fixing agent), Finishing chemical allocation (hand feel, drape, luster), Print ink allocation (Pantone match, viscosity, fastness), Hot-stamp foil allocation (color, finish, adhesion), Edge-paint / edge-stitch thread allocation. Material-sourcing target: 2-3 working days for stock items, 5-7 working days for special-order. The 8-color-lab-dip cycle covers 8 elements: Lab-dip submission (3-5 colors per SKU), Spectrophotometer measurement (Delta E 2000), Customer-side review (digital + physical), Lab-dip revision (1-2 rounds), Strike-off on production substrate, Light-box comparison (D65, TL84, UV), Final color approval (digital sign-off), Master color archive (recipe, process, instrument-reading). Lab-dip cycle target: 5-7 working days per round, 2 rounds typical. The 7-and-8 material-and-color is the operating spine.

Section 3 — The 6-Sample-Approval Gate & 9-Pre-Production-PPAP Layer

The 6-sample-approval gate covers 6 elements: Pre-production sample (production substrate), Hand-feel approval (touch, drape, luster), Print registration approval (Delta E, registration), Hot-stamp / emboss approval (adhesion, depth), Edge / hemming approval (straightness, fraying), Final sample sign-off (customer portal). Sample-approval target: 3-5 working days from lab-dip to pre-production-sample sign-off. The 9-pre-production-ppap layer covers 9 elements: Production routing (machine, line, operator), Process parameter setting (temperature, speed, tension), Initial run (50-200m production sample), In-process measurement (color, GSM, width, edge), Final inspection (AQL, Delta E, fastness), PPAP documentation (process flow, control plan, FMEA), Customer PPAP approval (PSW, ISIR), Pre-shipment sample (PSI) reservation, Production order release (lot size, delivery date). PPAP target: 5-7 working days from sample sign-off to production release. The 6-and-9 sample-to-PPAP is the operating spine.

Section 4 — The 8-Production-Line Setup-SMED & 11-In-Process-Routing

The 8-production-line setup-smed covers 8 elements: Changeover kit pre-stage (SMED single-minute exchange of die), Quick-release fixture (clamp, jig, mandrel), Color-change optimization (5-min color swap, 1-min water flush), Width-change optimization (2-min width swap), Tooling pre-stage (knife, roller, emboss die), Operator cross-training (multi-skill, multi-line), Setup-time reduction tracking (Kaizen), Setup-time target (under 15 minutes per changeover). SMED target: setup-time reduction from 60-90 min to 12-18 min per changeover. The 11-in-process-routing covers 11 elements: Warp / weft preparation (yarn, tension, beam), Weaving (rapier, air-jet, water-jet loom), Greige inspection (visual, defect map), Pre-treatment (scouring, bleaching, mercerizing), Dyeing (jet, beam, jigger, continuous), Color matching (Delta E, lab, instrument), Stenter finishing (width, GSM, hand feel), Printing (digital, screen, rotary, hot-stamp, emboss), Edge / hemming (heat-cut, fold, stitch, paint), In-process AQL sampling (every 500m, every lot), Final AQL sampling (lot release). In-process-routing target: 12-18 working days for 50,000m typical order. The 8-and-11 production-routing is the operating spine.

Section 5 — The 7-Post-Finishing-AQL & 9-Packing-Cartonization

The 7-post-finishing-aql covers 7 elements: 100% piece-by-piece visual at rewind, AQL sampling per ISO 2859-1 (Level II, 1.0/2.5/4.0), Lab-test suite (color Delta E, crocking, light-fastness, tensile, pH, formaldehyde, heavy metal, OEKO-TEX), Visual defect count (snag, slub, hole, fray, print misregistration), Packing / labeling / barcode check, Pre-shipment PSI report (photos, certificate, AQL data), Customer portal upload (PSI report, photos, certificate). Post-finishing-AQL target: 1-2 working days from production completion to PSI sign-off. The 9-packing-cartonization covers 9 elements: Carton specification (FSC, recycled-content, mono-material), Inner pack (tissue, belly-band, sticker, hangtag), Master pack (corrugated shipper, partition, dunnage), Palletization (size, weight, stretch-wrap, edge-protection), Container loading plan (cartonization, weight distribution), Barcode / QR / NFC label (SKU, lot, DPP link), Country-of-origin label (required by 38 markets), Retailer-specific label (Walmart, Target, Costco, etc.), Documentation pack (CI, PL, COO, Cert, DPP, ZDHC, GRS, FSC).
