Section 1 — The 9-CSR-Compliance Layer & 11-RPET-Recycled-Content Stack

The 9-csr-compliance layer covers 9 elements: Walmart Project Gigaton (1B-ton CO2e reduction by 2030), Target Forward (own-brand 100% recycled / sustainable by 2030), Sephora Clean+Planet Positive, L'Oréal LRP (L'Oréal for the Future), Inditex Join Life, H&M Conscious Collection, Marks & Spencer Plan A, Costco Sustainability Commitment, Amazon Climate Pledge Friendly. Each program has 5-7 KPIs: recycled content, carbon footprint, water, chemical, packaging, social audit, traceability. The 11-rpet-recycled-content stack covers 11 elements: Post-consumer recycled PET flake, Pre-consumer recycled PET chip, Ocean-bound plastic (OBP), GRS-certified recycled yarn, RCS-certified recycled content, Recycled-content claim (chain-of-custody), GRS label issuance, RCS label issuance, OBP certification (Zero Plastic Oceans), Pre-consumer waste stream (yarn waste, selvedge), Recycled-content mass-balance accounting. Smith Ribbon offers 30%, 50%, 75%, and 100% RPET options with GRS / RCS / OBP certificate per shipment. The 9-and-11 compliance-and-recycled-content is the operating spine.

Section 2 — The 7-Bamboo-Fiber Line & 8-Low-Carbon-Dye Process

The 7-bamboo-fiber line covers 7 elements: FSC bamboo pulp, Bamboo viscose (closed-loop solvent), Bamboo lyocell (TENCEL-like), Bamboo-cotton blend, Bamboo-PLA blend, Bamboo-charcoal-infused yarn, Bamboo-certificate (FSC, OCS, GOTS). Bamboo's edge: fast-renewable (3-5 year cycle), low-pesticide, biodegradable. The 8-low-carbon-dye process covers 8 elements: Low-temperature disperse dye (98C vs 130C), Supercritical CO2 dyeing (no water), Air-dye (DyeCoo, no water, no effluent), Digital printing (water-based pigment ink), Foam-dye (50% water reduction), Pad-batch cold-pad-batch (energy saving), Natural / plant-based dye (indigo, madder, walnut, tea), Low-carbon dye certification (ZDHC, bluesign, GOTS). Target carbon reduction vs conventional: 35-60% on air-dye, 25-40% on low-temp disperse. The 7-and-8 bio-fiber-and-dye-chemistry is the operating spine.

Section 3 — The 6-Energy-Renewable Mix & 9-Water-Circular System

The 6-energy-renewable mix covers 6 elements: Rooftop solar PV, On-site wind (where viable), Grid-purchased renewable energy certificate (REC / I-REC / GO), Solar PPA (power purchase agreement), Biomass / process-heat recovery, Green-power certificate retirement. Target: 60-100% renewable by 2030. The 9-water-circular system covers 9 elements: Incoming water meter, Reverse-osmosis (RO) reuse, Dye-bath reuse (counter-current), Wash-water reclaim, Stenter condensate capture, Cooling-tower blowdown reuse, Effluent treatment plant (ETP) biological + MBR, ZLD (zero liquid discharge) on premium tier, Discharge quality (COD <80 ppm, BOD <30 ppm, pH 6-9, TDS <2000 ppm). Target: 50-80% water recycle by 2028. The 6-and-9 energy-and-water sustainability is the operating spine.

Section 4 — The 7-Chemical-ZDHC-MRSL Layer & 9-Social-Audit SMETA-BSCI Layer

The 7-chemical-zdhc-mrsl layer covers 7 elements: ZDHC MRSL 3.1 conformance, ZDHC InCheck verification (Level 1-3), bluesign certification, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 (Class I-IV), GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard), REACH SVHC compliance (<0.1% w/w), Chemical inventory transparency (SDS, exposure). Smith Ribbon maintains ZDHC InCheck Level 2 across all 12 manufacturing sites. The 9-social-audit smeta-bsci layer covers 9 elements: SMETA 4-Pillar (Labor, H&S, Environment, Business Ethics), BSCI (Business Social Compliance Initiative), Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit, Fair-Wage certification, No-Child-Labor verification, No-Forced-Labor verification, Freedom of Association, Working-Hours compliance (60hr/wk max), Grievance mechanism. Annual on-site SMETA 4-Pillar audit + quarterly desktop review across 12 sites. The 7-and-9 chemical-and-social is the operating spine.

The 8-esg-scorecard-csrd layer covers 8 elements: ESRS E1 Climate change (Scope 1/2/3), ESRS E2 Pollution, ESRS E3 Water & marine resources, ESRS E4 Biodiversity & ecosystems, ESRS E5 Resource use & circular economy, ESRS S1 Own workforce, ESRS S2 Workers in value chain, ESRS G1 Business conduct. Annual disclosure aligned with EU CSRD / ESRS double-materiality assessment. The 11-and-8 safety-and-disclosure is the operating spine.

Section 6 — The 9-DPP-Digital-Product-Passport & 6-Carbon-Footprint Scope-3 Layer

The 9-dpp-digital-product-passport covers 9 elements: Material composition (fiber, dye, finish), Recycled content %, Manufacturing facility (mill, line, date), Carbon footprint (kgCO2e/m), Water consumption (L/m), Chemical inventory (ZDHC, REACH, OEKO-TEX), Social-audit attestation, End-of-life pathway (recycle, compost, energy recovery), QR code / NFC link to passport registry. EU DPP mandatory by 2030 on textile products. Smith Ribbon is live on 100% of EU shipments. The 6-carbon-footprint scope-3 layer covers 6 elements: Category 1 Purchased goods (raw fiber, dye, chemical), Category 4 Upstream transportation (incoming), Category 10 Processing of sold products (mill), Category 11 Use of sold products, Category 12 End-of-life treatment, Category 9 Downstream transportation.
