8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access);

  • Ask us to delete your data (erasure / "right to be forgotten");

To exercise any of these rights, email us at xmmsd@126.com. We respond within 30 days. California residents also have the right not to sell or share their personal information — we do neither. If you are unsatisfied, you may complain to your local data protection authority (for example the ICO in the UK, or a supervisory authority in the EU).
