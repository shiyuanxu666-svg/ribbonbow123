Each country carries a per-tier envelope (tier-1: USD 4M to USD 18M, tier-2: USD 0.8M to USD 4.5M, tier-3: USD 0.18M to USD 1.2M) backed by Euler-Hermes, Atradius, and Coface trade-credit insurance. Each milestone unlocks an incremental 12 basis-point margin-ratchet on the working-capital facility, totaling 72 basis-points across all six milestones. The architecture unlocks 4 to 11 percent program-lifetime margin through the ESG-linked margin-ratchet and consistently delivers 32K USD to 88K USD per-incident cost-recovery during supply-shock events.
