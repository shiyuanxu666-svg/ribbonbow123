1. Credential 1-6 — Chemical Compliance Family: OEKO-TEX, REACH, CPSIA, Prop-65, ZDHC, MRSL

Credential 1 is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 where the auditor reviews the OEKO-TEX certificate scope, class (I baby / II skin-contact / III decorative / IV furnishing), renewal-cycle, and lab-tested fiber / dye / chemical / finish conformance to the OEKO-TEX Annex 4 / Annex 6 limit-value list. Credential 2 is EU REACH SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern) where the auditor reviews the SVHC-declaration, the 235-entry ECHA-candidate list, the 235-entry Annex XVII restricted-substance list, and the SDS (Safety Data Sheet) batch-level traceability across dye / chemical / finish inputs. Credential 3 is US CPSIA (Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act) where the auditor reviews lead-content <100 ppm and phthalate-content <0.1 percent test certificates for products intended for users under age 12. Credential 4 is California Proposition 65 where the auditor reviews the 900-plus chemical warning-list conformance, including formaldehyde / benzene / lead / cadmium / phthalates. Credential 5 is ZDHC (Zero Discharge of Hazardous Chemicals) where the auditor reviews the wastewater-treatment sludge-conformance and the ZDHC-MRSL (Manufacturing Restricted Substances List) for textile / leather / footwear chemistry. Credential 6 is the brand-specific MRSL (Manufacturing Restricted Substances List) where the auditor validates the brand-buyer private-label program's restricted-substance list beyond ZDHC baseline. Chemical-compliance under-specification triggers up to 84,000 USD recall-cost per SKU when the EU RAPEX or US CPSC issues a consumer-alert for SVHC non-conformance.
