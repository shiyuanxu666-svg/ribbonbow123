19-Stage Q1 2027 NPI 90-Day Speed-to-Market Gantt — From Concept to First-DC-Receipt

The single most expensive mistake in B2B ribbon OEM 2026 H2 launch-readiness is to run the artwork sign-off, the sample approval, the production planning, the QA, and the logistics as 5 sequential stages — that is the 180-to-220-day legacy Gantt. The 152-module architecture runs them as 4 parallel tracks, compressing the 180-to-220-day legacy Gantt to a 90-to-110-day Q1 2027 launch Gantt.

The 19 stages fall into 4 parallel tracks: Track A — Artwork & Color (Stage 1-6) (concept brief, brand-asset handoff, artwork rider generation, Pantone PMS color approval, color-locked substrate swatch, artwork sign-off), Track B — Sample & Tooling (Stage 7-11) (lab-dip submission, hand-sample submission, production-sample submission, die-tooling sign-off, pre-production-sample sign-off), Track C — Production & QA (Stage 12-15) (production calendar lock, greige-lot commitment, dye-lot commitment, inline QA + AQL + photo-evidence), Track D — Logistics & Launch (Stage 16-19) (cartonization, container-loading, ocean-freight booking, DC-receipt confirmation, launch-go-live).
