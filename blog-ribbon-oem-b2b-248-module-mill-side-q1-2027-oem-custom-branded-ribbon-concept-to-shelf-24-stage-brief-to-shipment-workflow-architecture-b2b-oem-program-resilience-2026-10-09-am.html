1. Concept-to-Stage-Gate 0 (Stages 1-3) — Brief Lock, Artwork Start, Pantone Triage

Stage 1 is a one-page 'design brief lock' that names the SKU family, target retail price-band, channel (mass / premium / luxury), target ship-date, MOQ tier, and acceptance criteria for color, hand-feel, width tolerance, repeat-length, and end-use contact (direct-skin, gift-pack, infant, food-contact). Stage 2 is artwork start, where the mill-side design desk handles vector-illustration trace, Pantone-to-dye-recipe pre-mapping, and substrate-feasibility review (RPET, cotton, satin, grosgrain, organza, velvet). Stage 3 is Pantone triage, where the mill runs the lab-dip calculator against the customer's Pantone TCX/TPG book, flags 'in-gamut, near, far' colors, and requests a Pantone Plus subscription token if not already on file. Without these three stages locked before week 2 of the 90-day runway, every downstream stage slips.

2. Sample-to-Tooling-Stage-Gate 1 (Stages 4-7) — Lab Dip, Strike-Off, Cylinder Engrave, Die-Cut

Stage 4 is lab dip, with three submissions in 7-10 working days using the agreed Pantone TCX/TPG codes. Stage 5 is strike-off, where the mill runs 1-2 meters of substrate through the planned print process (rotary-screen, hot-foil-stamp, digital-print, jacquard-weave) for the brand to physically approve hand-feel, color-on-substrate, edge-finish. Stage 6 is cylinder engrave / digital-plate burn for rotary-screen jobs, or tooling for die-cut shapes (rosette, pre-tied-bow, gift-tag). Stage 7 is die-set or print-cylinder proof, signed off on a Pantone-CO-routed approval sheet. These four stages finish by week 5 of the runway.

3. Pre-Production-Stage-Gate 2 (Stages 8-12) — PPAP, Bulk Yarn, Dye Lot, Greige Weave, Finish

Stage 8 is PPAP (production-part-approval-process) submission with capacity-confirm, lead-time-confirm, color-tolerance window, AQL-agreement, and defect-classification matrix. Stage 9 is bulk-yarn PO drop to upstream yarn-supplier (RPET, polyester, cotton, bamboo, metallic). Stage 10 is dye-lot reservation at the dye-house, with lot-to-lot color-continuity tracking via spectrophotometer readings (CIELab Delta-E ≤ 1.0 within-lot, ≤ 1.5 lot-to-lot). Stage 11 is greige-weave on the mill-side looms, with picks-per-cm, ends-per-cm, and weight-gsm captured per lot. Stage 12 is finish — heat-setting, calendaring, softening, water-repellent, fire-retardant as per spec.

4. Inline-Quality-and-Capacity-Stage-Gate 3 (Stages 13-17) — Inline AOI, Cut, Sew, Finish-Lines, Pack

Stage 13 is inline-AOI vision-inspection, where the mill's machine-vision cameras flag yarn-slubs, dye-streaks, misprints, edge-curl, off-color-tray at the same speed as the loom (closed-loop defect detection architecture detailed in the mill-side AI-vision playbook). Stage 14 is cut-to-width on the slitter-rewinder, with running footage captured. Stage 15 is sew / bow-fold / pre-tie (for pre-made bows). Stage 16 is finish-lines (iron-on-back, hot-cut, wired-edge-tip). Stage 17 is pack, with co-packing options including spooling, flat-fold, bow-card, header-card, EAN/UPC labeling, retail-ready-carton. These five stages finish by week 8.

5. Lab-Test-and-Compliance-Stage-Gate 4 (Stages 18-21) — OEKO-TEX, REACH, CPSIA, RSL, DPP

Stage 18 is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I (infant) or Class II (direct-skin) submit, with 14-day test-cycle. Stage 19 is REACH SVHC, CPSIA, Prop 65, ZDHC MRSL compliance labs. Stage 20 is retailer-specific RSL (restricted-substance-list) — Walmart, Target, Costco, Tesco, Carrefour, Aldi all have their own lists that the mill must clear. Stage 21 is Digital-Product-Passport (DPP) data-capture under EU ESPR 2026/2026 — fiber-source, manufacturing-lot, recycled-content % (GRS / RCS), supply-chain mapping, carbon-footprint. These four stages finish by week 10.

6. Ship-and-Restock-Stage-Gate 5 (Stages 22-24) — Pre-Shipment AQL, Container Pack, EDI/VMI Handoff

Stage 22 is pre-shipment AQL inspection per ISO 2859-1, with accept/reject based on the agreed critical-major-minor AQL. Stage 23 is container pack — inner-poly-bag + desiccant + export-carton + pallet + container-load-plan, with VMI-ready barcoded cartons and EDI-856 ASN. Stage 24 is handoff — EDI/VMI signal to the brand-buyer 3PL/DC, retailer-vendor-managed-inventory signal, and post-shipment chargeback-defensible documentary pack (COO, CI, PL, B/L, COO-Certificate-of-Conformance, OEKO-TEX certificate, DPP export). The 90-day runway closes with the container on the vessel.
