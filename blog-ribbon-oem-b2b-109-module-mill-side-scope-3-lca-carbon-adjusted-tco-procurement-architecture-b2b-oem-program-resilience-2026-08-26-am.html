2. The 14-Stage Scope-3 LCA Boundary Mapping

The 14-stage Scope-3 LCA boundary map forces the mill to disclose, item-by-item, the cradle-to-gate emissions: (1) raw-yarn polymerization, (2) raw-yarn spinning, (3) dye-and-chemical synthesis, (4) weaving-and-knitting energy, (5) finishing-and-heat-set energy, (6) printing-and-ink solvent, (7) slitting-and-spooling energy, (8) carton-and-pallet, (9) outbound-trucking to port, (10) ocean-or-air line-haul fuel, (11) destination-port handling, (12) destination-inland trucking, (13) DC-or-3PL storage, (14) end-of-life packaging disposal. Each stage is mapped to a GHG-Protocol Scope-3 category (1, 3, 4, 9, 11, 12), and a mill whose boundary omits a stage is flagged on the scorecard.
