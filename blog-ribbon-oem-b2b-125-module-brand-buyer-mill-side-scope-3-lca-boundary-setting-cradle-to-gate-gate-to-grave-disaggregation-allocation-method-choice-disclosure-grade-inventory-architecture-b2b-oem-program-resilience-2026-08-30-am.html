How Smith Ribbon Operationalises the 125-Module Mill-Side Scope-3 LCA Boundary-Setting, Cradle-to-Gate & Gate-to-Grave Disaggregation, Allocation Method Choice & Disclosure-Grade Inventory Program — 11-Step Audit, 6-Stakeholder RACI, 14-Clause Rider, 7-Stage Ladder, Quarterly Recalc

Smith Ribbon runs the 125-module mill-side scope-3 LCA boundary-setting, cradle-to-gate & gate-to-grave disaggregation, allocation method choice & disclosure-grade inventory program as a quarterly cadence. Step 1 Functional-Unit-Definition: the team defines functional-unit as 1 meter of finished ribbon (or 1 SKU per kilogram) for every brand-buyer program and locks the reference-flow in the LCA-software-platform. Step 2 System-Boundary-Map: the team maps cradle-to-gate (raw-material extraction → fibre production → yarn spinning → weaving → dyeing → finishing → slitting → packing → mill gate) plus gate-to-grave (mill gate → distribution → retail → consumer-use → end-of-life) and documents every flow in the system-boundary diagram. Step 3 Cut-Off-Rule-Definition: the team applies a 1% mass, 1% energy, 1% environmental cut-off rule, documents every cut-off decision, and exposes the cut-off table to the brand-buyer. Step 4 Co-Product-Handling: the team identifies every co-product (e.g. selvedge, off-cut, recycled-PET-flake), applies mass-allocation, economic-allocation, or system-expansion per ISO-14044, and documents the choice. Step 5 Recycled-Content & Bio-Attributed: the team credits recycled-content and bio-attributed inputs per ISO-14021 / ISO-14067 and GRS / FSC, and exposes the recycled-content certificate to the brand-buyer. Step 6 Energy / Water / Emission Allocation: the team allocates energy, water, and emissions by mass, economic, or causal basis and exposes the allocation table. Step 7 Primary-Data-Collection: the team collects primary energy, water, material, waste, emission, and packaging data from every mill station, MES, and SCADA system and pushes it into the LCA-software-platform. Step 8 Secondary-Data-Fallback: when primary data is unavailable, the team falls back to ecoinvent / GaBi secondary data and discloses the secondary-data share. Step 9 Data-Quality-Score: the team computes a data-quality-score per technology-coverage, time-coverage, geography-coverage, and primary-data-share, and exposes the score to the brand-buyer. Step 10 Assurance-Level: the team commissions a third-party limited-assurance or reasonable-assurance review per ISO-14064-3 and exposes the assurance opinion. Step 11 CSRD / ESRS Mapping & Quarterly Recalc: the team maps every LCA output to CSRD-ESRS-E1, CSRD-ESRS-E5, EU-CBAM, CDP-Climate, and SBTi, recalculates the inventory every quarter, and publishes the new disclosure.
