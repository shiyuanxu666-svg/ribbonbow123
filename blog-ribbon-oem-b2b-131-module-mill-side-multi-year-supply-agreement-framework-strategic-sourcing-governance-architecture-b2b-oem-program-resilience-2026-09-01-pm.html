Why 12-month PO-driven sourcing is structurally suboptimal in 2026

Three structural forces make PO-driven sourcing a margin tax, not a procurement strategy. First, the Section-301 list-4A and 4B tariff regime, plus the EU-CBAM perimeter, turn every 90-day repricing window into a vendor-margin-extraction opportunity that the brand absorbs. Second, the post-pandemic capacity-pre-booking reality, especially for Q4 holiday-peak, means a mill that does not have a 12-to-36-month capacity-reservation contract will prioritize spot-paying customers, and the brand gets de-prioritized. Third, the cost-of-switching a B2B ribbon OEM vendor — artwork, color-library, tooling, packaging-spec, EDI integration, payment-term re-set — averages 4 to 11 percent of annual spend, and PO-driven sourcing forces brands to re-pay that switching cost every 2 to 3 years.
