1. Stage 1-3 — Brief-Ingest, Asset-Normalization, and Color-Intent-Translation as the Co-Design Foundation

Stage 1 (brief-ingest) captures the brand-owner creative brief, the merchandising specification, the seasonal color-story, and the prior-season archive into a structured data-record. Stage 2 (asset-normalization) converts the deliverable assets — Pantone-FHI chips, RGB hex-codes, CMYK sample-tone-targets, fabric-substrate photos, and competitor-benchmark swatches — into a normalized asset-library with delta-E compatibility metadata. Stage 3 (color-intent-translation) runs AI-augmented Pantone-FHI conversion, fabric-substrate compensation, and substrate-aware delta-E prediction so the brand-equity color intent survives the substrate-shift from Cotton-jersey to Polyester-satin to Velvet-pile to Wire-edged-organza. For Q1-2027 brand-owner programs, Stage 1 compresses brief-intake from 6 days to 1.5 days, Stage 2 compresses asset-normalization from 4 days to 1 day, and Stage 3 lifts color-intent-translation first-pass-right from 64 percent to 89 to 94 percent across the FY2026 to FY2028 horizon.

2. Stage 4-6 — Virtual-Swatch-Render, AI-Visual-Library, and Substrate-Aware Delta-E-Prediction

Stage 4 (virtual-swatch-render) generates a 4-axis substrate simulation (polyester satin, velvet-pile, wire-edged organza, RPET-grosgrain) with AI-augmented sheen, drape, and light-handling prediction so the brand-owner merchandising team can sign off on color-direction before mill-side swatch-fabrication consumes yarn inventory and calendar time. Stage 5 (AI-visual-library) sequences 1,400 to 6,000 historical swatch-records with substrate, dye-class, fixation-method, and Pantone-FHI metadata so the mill-side color-stewardship team can match a brand-equity color-intent against prior-season archive in 22 minutes instead of 5 hours. Stage 6 (substrate-aware delta-E-prediction) runs 9 measurement-point delta-E simulation, light-source-illumination compensation (D65, D50, A, F11), and observer-angle compensation (10-degree, 2-degree) so the predicted swatch matches the mill-side fabricated swatch within delta-E 0.8 to 1.2 across 89 to 94 percent of substrates.

3. Stage 7-9 — Mill-Side-Swatch-Fabrication, Co-Design-Review, and Approval-Lock as the Architecture Outcome

Stage 7 (mill-side-swatch-fabrication) sequences dye-laboratory lab-dip, pilot-line sample, production-line pre-production-sample (PPS), and master-reference-standard fabrication under the print-finish specification sheet (PFSS) signed off in Stage 3. Stage 8 (co-design-review) pairs the brand-owner merchandising team, the mill-side color-stewardship team, and the QA-laboratory team in a structured 4-stage review meeting (virtual-render review, lab-dip review, PPS review, master-standard review) with delta-E-tolerance documentation, wash-fastness benchmark, light-fastness benchmark, and crock-test benchmark attached. Stage 9 (approval-lock) freezes the master-standard, archives the digital-asset to the AI-visual-library, version-controls the substrate-bill-of-materials in mill-side ERP, and triggers the production-ramp for the next 6 to 14 weeks of replenishment-cascade. For Q1-2027 brand-owner programs, Stage 7 compresses swatch-fabrication from 18 days to 9 days, Stage 8 compresses co-design-review from 4 review-meetings over 11 days to 4 review-meetings over 5 days, and Stage 9 lifts approval-lock first-pass-right from 58 percent to 86 to 91 percent.

4. AI-Visual-Library Curation, Color-Stewardship-Cadence, and Substrate-Library Indexing

The AI-visual-library is curated monthly with 80 to 160 new historical swatch-records sequenced into the substrate-index, the dye-class-index, the Pantone-FHI-index, the season-index, and the brand-owner-index so the color-stewardship team can query 14,000-19,000 records in 22 minutes with delta-E-tolerance pre-filtering. The color-stewardship cadence runs on a 14-day review-meeting cycle (Wednesdays at 09:00 CST) with the mill-side color-stewardship team, the lab-supervisor, the QA-laboratory team, the merchandising-controller, and the brand-owner merchandising representative. The substrate-library indexes 38 to 64 substrate-codes (polyester satin 75D, polyester satin 50D, velvet-pile 120G, wire-edged organza 60G, RPET-grosgrain 38G, etc.) with hand-feel-rating, light-handling-rating, wash-fastness-rating, and Pantone-FHI delta-E-tolerance documented for each. For Q1-2027 brand-owner programs, the curation cadence lifts library-first-pass-right by 14 to 22 percentage points and the substrate-library indexing lifts substrate-shift first-pass-right by 18 to 26 percentage points.

5. Pantone-FHI Translation-Engine, Delta-E-Tolerance-Calibration, and Light-Source-Compensation

The Pantone-FHI translation-engine runs 9,400 to 14,000 Pantone-FHI-chip-to-substrate translations per Q1-2027 quarter with delta-E-tolerance-calibration (delta-E less than 1.0 for premium-tier, delta-E less than 1.5 for value-tier), light-source-compensation (D65 primary, D50 secondary, A and F11 tertiary), and substrate-aware sheen-prediction so the brand-equity color-intent survives substrate-shift with 89 to 94 percent first-pass-right. The delta-E-tolerance-calibration is benchmarked against 4,800 to 7,200 historical mill-side lab-dip records with spectrophotometric Lab-delta-E, CMC delta-E, and CIE 2000 delta-E triple-validation. The light-source-compensation is benchmarked against 1,400 to 2,600 retail-floor light-source records (D65 daylight, D50 store-ambient, A tungsten, F11 fluorescent) so the predicted swatch matches the retail-display rendering within delta-E 1.0 across 86 to 91 percent of store-ambient conditions.
