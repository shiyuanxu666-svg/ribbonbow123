19-Stage Cradle-to-Gate LCA Boundary — From Raw-Yarn to Slit-Lot Ribbon

The single most expensive mistake in B2B ribbon OEM Q1 2027 PCF disclosure is to set the LCA boundary at the mill-gate only. The EU CBAM, the US Buy Clean Initiative, and the Q1 2027 brand-buyer ESG scorecards all demand cradle-to-gate. The 155-module architecture deploys a 19-stage cradle-to-gate LCA boundary: Stage 1 PET-Chip Feedstock Extraction, Stage 2 PTA / MEG Synthesis, Stage 3 Polymerization, Stage 4 Yarn Spinning, Stage 5 Yarn-Draw Texturizing, Stage 6 Greige-Fabric Weaving, Stage 7 Pre-Treatment Scouring, Stage 8 Dyeing, Stage 9 Stentering / Heat-Setting, Stage 10 Printing, Stage 11 Finishing (softener, anti-static), Stage 12 Slitting, Stage 13 Cartonization, Stage 14 Inner-Pack, Stage 15 Outer-Pack, Stage 16 Palletization, Stage 17 Containerization, Stage 18 Mill-Gate Handoff, Stage 19 Inbound-Logistics Allocation (buyer-side).

End-state: 19-stage boundary with 9 milestone gates, 5 emission-source categories, 3-stakeholder (EHS, finance, procurement) alignment.
